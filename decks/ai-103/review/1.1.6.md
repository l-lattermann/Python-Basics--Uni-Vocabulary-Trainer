# 1.1.6 Responsible AI

Course quality: clear — six principles, each with a definition sentence and a concrete example or practice; light on how to apply them in Foundry, which later modules cover.
Cards: 9 (basic 7, mc 2, order 0). Additions: 0.
- No images: the six pictures (scales, shield, padlock, people, eye, handshake) are decorative icons.
- The "why responsible AI matters more for AI" intro (probabilistic models, over-trust in human-like AI) has no card of its own; it sits in the explanations of 1.1.6-01 and 1.1.6-07.
- mc 1.1.6-02 (fairness vs inclusiveness) and 1.1.6-08 (transparency vs privacy) target the two pairs the exam is most likely to confuse.
- No neighbouring unit of module 1.1 covers the principles; the module assessment (1.1.9) asks nothing about them.
